## What we do

SKMC Global is a regulatory compliance consultancy based in India. We get
wireless device manufacturers WPC/ETA (Equipment Type Approval) certification —
the mandatory approval from India's Wireless Planning & Coordination wing
for any product containing Bluetooth, WiFi, Zigbee, or any RF transmitter.

## What we handle end-to-end

1. **RF test coordination** — scheduling and managing testing at
   WPC-accredited labs (per ETSI EN 300 328 for 2.4 GHz BLE/WiFi devices
   and applicable standards for other bands)
2. **Technical documentation** — preparation and pre-audit of the complete
   filing package: RF test report review, user manual compliance, RF
   datasheet, authorization letter, product photos and labels
3. **WPC portal filing** — end-to-end application on the Saral Sanchar
   portal, including fee payment and class license / license category
   determination (ETA for de-licensed bands; WPC license for licensed bands)
4. **Follow-up & approval tracking** — direct liaison with WPC officers,
   query responses, and delivery of the final ETA certificate
5. **Post-approval support** — label compliance guidance (e.g., "ETA
   number must appear on product/label"), renewals, and new-SKU variants

## Turnaround

ETA approval in ⚠️ VERIFY: 4–6 weeks with us, vs 3–6 months typical
self-filing or inexperienced consultant route. The biggest time killers we
eliminate: incomplete documentation (top rejection reason), wrong test
standard selection, and portal query back-and-forth.

## Why clients choose us over DIY or big-firm routes

- **Speed:** parallel test-lab scheduling + pre-audit means no rejection loops
- **Flat scope:** one engagement covers testing → portal → certificate,
  no per-stage billing surprises
- **Device-category fluency:** we know which radio module certifications
  (module-level ETA vs system-level ETA) apply — avoids over-testing costs

## What we do NOT do (so the LLM doesn't over-qualify)

- We are NOT a testing lab — we coordinate with accredited labs
- We do NOT handle telecom service licenses (VNO/UL/VNO-ISP) — equipment
  approval only
- We do NOT handle BIS/CRS, EPR, or TEC/MTCTE — those are separate SKMC
  service lines with their own campaigns
